## 🇨🇳 中文

只用用户提供的清晰商品图，制作一条商品主导的三维质感广告；C4D 指视觉完成度，不代表实际使用软件或交付可编辑工程。流程先识别产品与可靠视觉事实，再写并展示时间码脚本等待确认，确认后一次生成完整视频与原生声音。发生冲突时按固定优先级取舍：商品身份和包装文字准确、商品清晰可见、材质和运动可信、广告信息明确，最后才加运镜与特效。不虚构卖点，不静默拆分一次生成任务。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 清晰商品图 → 一份已确认的时间码脚本，外加一条完整视频与原生音乐音效
- 商品身份保真优先：保留商品、Logo、标签布局、数量、可见符号与已知规格
- 选择一种产品材质模式（如饮品／液体／化妆品等），按有物理原因的动作设计材质表现
- 声音可选音乐＋音效、纯音乐、含旁白或无声；字幕只在明确要求时添加
- 商业广告默认保留至少一个有意设计的传播文案节点，只用用户提供或确认过的真实卖点
- 不交付可编辑 C4D 工程，不虚构卖点，不静默拆分一次生成任务

## 🇬🇧 English

Builds one product-led, 3D-textured commercial from the clear product image you already have; C4D describes visual finish, not the software used or an editable project delivered. The flow first identifies the product and reliable visual facts, then writes and shows a timecode script for approval, and after approval generates the full video with native sound in one pass. Conflicts are resolved by a fixed priority: product identity and packaging-text accuracy, product visibility, believable material and motion, clear ad message — camera work and effects come last. It never invents selling points and never silently splits one generation task.

### ✨ What it does

- Clear product image → an approved timecode script plus one complete video with native music and effects
- Product identity comes first: product, logo, label layout, quantity, visible symbols and known specs are preserved
- Pick one product material mode (drink/liquid, cosmetics and more) and drive motion with physically motivated actions
- Sound can be music + effects, music only, with narration, or silent; subtitles are added only on request
- Commercials keep at least one deliberate messaging text node by default, using only confirmed real selling points
- No editable C4D project, no invented claims, no silently split generation
